PROJECT GIYA STEP-BY-STEP HOW TO NAVIGATE INSTRUCTIONS:

  1. Open your browser. Type tinyurl.com/agnorlrmcsite to access the ADN-LILRAP – LRMS Initiated LR Access Point.
  2. Navigate and click "GIYA DRIVE" located beside "Home" on the menu.
  3. Use the Search Tab. In the user interface, you will find a search bar. Enter any keyword related to your subject, topic, or grade level that you want to check or download.
  4. Browse the results. Once you find the DLL (Daily Lesson Log) that you want to download, you're ready for the next step.
  5. Click the arrow button to download the DLL file.
  6. Download other resources. Click the amber folder button to download other resources related to the DLL, such as the PPT, BOW, or any other materials declared by the developer in the caption below the file name.
  7. After downloading, you can also customize the file depending on your preference.
